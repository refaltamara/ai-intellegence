Give me a campaign idea for my serum launch next month.
F

One idea, grounded in what the category actually rewarded in the last 60 days rather than in general best practice.

Run a dermatologist-led "barrier test" week on TikTok, then hand the format to mid-tier creators

Two phases: 5–7 dermatologist or pharmacist creators post a 7-day test with a shared before/after format, then 25–30 mid-tier creators reuse the format with their own results.

1Dermatologist-tier posts about serums drew 2.3× the comment rate of lifestyle creators across the category since July, and 64% of their comments are questions, which means purchase intent 4
2Skintific's wave last week used 27 creators but only 2 were derm-tier, so the expert lane is open for the next four weeks 1
3"Barrier" is the fastest-growing serum topic in comments this quarter, up from 9% to 23% of serum mentions, and your brand currently owns 4% of it 5
