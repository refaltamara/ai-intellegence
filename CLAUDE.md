Project: Fair Intel — AI marketing intelligence on Fair's social listening data (Indonesian beauty, TikTok + Instagram).

Read in this order before doing anything: docs/DECISIONS.md (overrides), docs/DATA_NOTES.md (what the data actually contains), docs/PRD.md (full spec), skills.registry.json (skill catalogue). UI reference: docs/prototype/fair-intel-prototype-v2.html.

Rules:
1. The model never computes numbers; skills and the query builder do, in SQL. Every skill result must include evidence; the runner rejects results without it.
2. skills.registry.json is the single source of truth for skills. UI, slash menu, and the run_skill tool definition are generated from it.
3. No raw SQL from model input. query_metrics is a whitelisted builder only.
4. All tables carry workspace_id. Brand ids are the canonical slugs in data/seed/brand_mapping_master.csv.
5. Tier and affiliate thresholds live in src/config; never hardcode. Bands are in docs/DECISIONS.md.
6. Instagram posts can belong to several brands; uniqueness is (platform, url, brand). Owned vs earned exists on TikTok only.
7. Skills whose data layer is missing return status "unavailable" with a plain message. velocity, forecast, narrative and the topic-based comment skills (objections, questions, dupes, claims, whitespace) are unavailable in v1. sentiment, comment-themes, drivers and seeding run wherever comments are loaded (profile workspaces first).
8. Stack: Next.js App Router + TypeScript, Drizzle, Neon Postgres, Vercel (Vercel Cron for agents), Resend email, Python/pandas ETL. Do not add Railway, pg-boss, or Redis.
9. Use `pnpm skill run <name>` to verify skills before touching UI. Verification: pnpm test, pnpm smoke, pnpm db:stats.
10. Do not integrate Fair Space or Fair Hub. Never commit secrets; .env is gitignored.

## Partner experience (docs/PRD_V2_PARTNER.md, v2.1 amendments)
- A workspace is a subject (a category panel of brands, or one profile such as an artist). The platform is "Fair Intelligence" everywhere; the assistant in Chats is CeMO on every team (DECISIONS, 30 Sep 2026). Persona and on-screen copy come from `workspaces.kind` + `settings` via `src/workspace/config.ts`. Every page and route scopes by `currentWorkspaceId()` (the session's workspace, or an owner's switch); never import DEFAULT_WORKSPACE_ID in `app/`. Nothing is shared across workspaces.
- People choose a team at sign-in (`/persona`: PR team or Brand & KOL team, one workspace each) and switch at the top of the sidebar. The MCP connector (`/api/mcp`, OAuth 2.1 + PKCE) scopes by its token's workspace, never the cookie; limits live in `src/config/mcp.ts`.
- Inside a conversation, skills are internal: never a skill name, slash command, tool name, or "Running…" in the thread or in model text. The server strips slashed skill names and logs mechanism_leak for anything else. The sidebar is Dashboard, Weekly Reports, Decks, Chats for a team with a brand panel, and Dashboard, Pulse, Chats, Reports for a PR team (`src/ui/Sidebar.tsx`); what CeMO can do opens from "/" or "+" in the composer (`src/skills/team.ts`, plain titles, picking fills an example question), with the library at `/skills`.
- Activity strings and result-card titles come from skills.registry.json (`activity`, `title`), templated from workspace counts, params_resolved and SkillResult.meta.
- The model asks at most one clarifying question per turn (ask_user; the turn ends on it and the next user message is its tool_result). No suggested follow-ups and no <counter> block (DECISIONS, 1 Oct 2026): a pushback is one plain sentence; a stray <followups> block is still swallowed by the stream. The evidence pane opens only when asked, charts only on "Show chart".
- Profile workspaces load through `etl/load_profile.py` with a contract in `etl/profiles/<workspace>.json` (owned handles, keywords, drop list). Comments carry `sentiment` (3 classes, labelled by `/api/cron/label` on Vercel); earned posts carry `stance`; owned posts and the subject's replies are never labelled. Off-topic contents are dropped at load and listed in the load report.
- Client brand is workspaces.client_brand_id (Fair's owners or data ops set it on the Data page, opened from the account row; default none).
- Each team opens on its Dashboard (`/dashboard`): fixed layout, filters only, every number from `src/dashboard/data.ts`; PR teams see the old Pulse view there. "Ask why" links carry a reference (`src/dashboard/askref.ts`), never numbers; the server re-reads the figures (`src/dashboard/ask.ts`) into a card and in front of the question. Pulses (`/pulse`, `src/pulses/`, PR teams; a panel team's `/pulse` redirects to Decks, its old boards still open) are boards: cards are Dashboard views with their own filters (numbers from `src/dashboard/data.ts` via `src/pulses/cards.ts`; `latest-month`/`latest-week` move with the data) or analyses pinned from Chats (`skill_run_id`, re-run on Refresh). Pure card metadata lives in `src/pulses/kinds.ts` so client components never import the data layer.
- Chats (`/`) is free-form. Decisions are retired (`/decisions`, `/d/*` redirect to Chats). Reports (`/reports`, `src/ui/ReportsHome.tsx`; off a panel team's sidebar, opened from Weekly Reports → Schedule) is one list: schedules with their reports, then reports from Chats (deck versions are not listed; their links open the deck); every report and schedule is deletable there. "Turn into a report" builds from the whole conversation (`src/reports/conversation.ts`). No pg-boss; no strict tools.
- The Weekly Competitor Pulse is a schedule (`agents.kind = 'weekly_report'`, contract in `params`; `src/competitor/scheduled.ts`): the model writes the words from a fact sheet (`src/competitor/write.ts`, `checkNarrative`, two retries, then the plain narrative); the .pptx and the .pdf are drawn from one layout (`buildDeck`; PDF via pdfkit in `src/competitor/pdfdeck.ts`, Liberation Sans in `assets/fonts`, no LibreOffice); files live in `report_files` and go out as email attachments. Weekly Reports (`/weekly`, `src/ui/weekly/`) shows the stored PDF slide by slide with Ask AI per slide: the browser sends only report + slide, the server reads `blocks.slides`/`blocks.sheet` (`src/reports/slideAsk.ts`). Since 1 Oct 2026 the deck carries the landscape (`src/competitor/landscape.ts` facts, `landscapeDeck.ts` slides: tiers, products, posting, close-ups, patterns) and three to five prioritised moves; Pulse's landscape cards read the same facts.
- The evidence pane: `src/chat/pane.ts` decides what opens it (rows, a chart with ≥3 points, an agent draft) and computes every number in a pane-action delta; the model only phrases them. Pane actions arrive as hidden user turns (`content_json.hidden`, `pane_action`); sort/filter live in `skill_runs.pane_state`. Exports go through `src/export/run.ts` (About sheet, pane state applied) and are logged in `exports`. Brand pages live at `/data/[brand]` (`src/brand/page.ts`): inventory only, five sections, no KPI hero, sounds deferred.
- Decks (`/decks`, DECISIONS 2 Oct 2026) are slide decks a panel team builds from a template (`src/decks/templates.ts`), from scratch, or from Chats ("Turn into a deck", "+ Add to a deck"; `src/decks/fromChat.ts`). A deck's `spec` (brands, optional client, grain week|month, platforms, slides, findings) becomes the weekly report's contract (`specContract`), so every number comes from `src/competitor/facts.ts`; the slide library is `src/competitor/slides.ts` (`deckSlides` decides what a report carries; the weekly report keeps its fixed set, plus the two caption slides after the drivers when the captions read name something), new slides in `libraryDeck.ts` (trend, top creators, top content, findings). Period words come from `src/competitor/period.ts`; monthly rules in `src/config/weekly.ts`. Each version is a report (`source = 'deck'`, `deck_id`) with files and `blocks.slides`/`sheet`, viewed in the Weekly Reports viewer with Ask AI. Findings re-run their skill over the version's period (`src/decks/findings.ts`). Recurring decks are made by `/api/cron/decks` (`src/decks/generate.ts`: skip a period already made, look again daily). The Weekly Competitor Pulse · Paragon stays separate, in Weekly Reports.
- Caption reading (DECISIONS 2 Oct 2026): `/api/cron/captions` tags posts in category workspaces that switched it on (`workspaces.settings.captions`) with product, event (+ name), offer, hook and angle (`posts.cap_*`; `src/captions/`). Posts with views over the floor (10K default) or from brand accounts, posted on or after `settings.captions.since` when set, one read per url. The model only names; slides (campaigns, angles; `src/competitor/captions.ts`; in decks and the Paragon weekly report) and query_metrics (`caption_*` dimensions and filters) count posts, creators and views over the tags in SQL.
- Listening workspaces (DECISIONS 3 Oct 2026): the complete listening schema is the template; `etl/load_listening.py <contract> <dump dir>` loads a Fair Listening dump (contract in `etl/listening/<workspace>.json`: brands grouped from per-platform handles, sentiment map). Owned vs earned on every platform there; comments arrive labelled (`sentiment_source = 'listening'`, five-point `sentiment_detail` and `csat` kept, `theme`, `topic_id`); snapshots run day 0 to 30. First: `fintech-id` (client GoPay).
- Roles (DECISIONS 3 Oct 2026): a workspace is the data, a role (`src/roles/model.ts`: PR, Brand & KOL, Social Media) is how the product behaves on it; teams are workspace × role (`settings.roles`, `fi_role` cookie, `currentRole()`), every role gets Dashboard, Decks, Chats. PR: the reputation dashboard (`src/reputation/dashboard.ts`), PR decks (`src/reputation/deck.ts`, templates with `family: "reputation"`, `spec.rep`), CeMO's comms-advisor voice. Social Media: the own-accounts dashboard (`src/social/dashboard.ts`; engagement is the measure, views only where reported, unmeasured posts out of medians), Social decks (`src/social/deck.ts`, `spec.social`), CeMO as content lead. `posts.relevant = false` (a listening post that does not name its brand) never counts: keep `p.relevant is not false` in any new posts query (`Where.workspace` and the dashboard's `scope` already do). Brand & KOL covers every platform a workspace holds (all = the workspace's platforms); own-account shares follow where own accounts are captured; the beauty product lexicon applies in beauty workspaces only.
- Roles as products (DECISIONS 4 Oct 2026): PR is Chorus, Brand & KOL is Atlas, Social Media is Spark, versioned major.minor in `role_versions`; a client's changes in `company_versions`, a person's in `personal_settings`. Read a role through `getRole(ws, role, user)` / `currentRole()` (`src/roles/store.ts`), never `ROLES[...]` in a request path; `src/roles/policy.ts` says what each layer may change. Only Refal or Rafli release (`pnpm role release`); role owners (Audia, Wega, Arneta) draft and roll back. A release never touches what a client built. Every model call goes through `anthropicClient({ workspace, purpose })` so its tokens land in `model_calls`.
- People (DECISIONS 4 Oct 2026): one `accounts` row per person (email, password, Fair duties in `staff`); a `users` row is a membership (account × workspace, `levels` per role: builder | member). Check permissions only with `can(actor, action, { workspace, role })` (`src/auth/can.ts`; `currentActor()`), never by the old owner/member role. Fair staff reach every workspace; clients reach their memberships. The CMS is `/admin` (staff only, 404 otherwise); a Builder's people page is `/team`; invitations go through `src/auth/invites.ts`.
- Role Lab (DECISIONS 4 Oct 2026): recipes (`src/recipes/`) are analyses as data over the query builder (posts and, since 4 Oct, comments); a role offers them via `recipes` and CeMO runs them with `run_recipe`; never edit a released recipe in place (new key). Every role has a test set (`src/roles/tests.ts`: guards, screens, golden questions; `pnpm role test`); a version is drafted, tested and proposed by role owners and released by Refal or Rafli, optionally to `stage_workspaces` first (`fairVersion(role, null, ws)` honours stages). The Lab's AI (`src/roles/lab.ts`) edits drafts only.
- Builders and Members (DECISIONS 4 Oct 2026): what a team makes (company skills as recipes, deck templates, house rules, facts, vocabulary) is one `creations` table (`src/company/creations.ts`): a Builder's is live when added, a Member's works for its maker and waits for a Builder. CeMO makes them with `make_skill`, `make_deck_template`, `remember_for_team`, and in Builder mode (Builders only) proposes changes with `change_team`; it never applies: every button goes through `/api/builder/*`, which checks `can()` and the guard rails. House rules and memory sit last in the prompt and never change a number (`src/company/rules.ts`). Dashboard sections are ids in `src/dashboard/sections.ts` (`tiles.*`); "Our Chorus" is `/company`; Client creations are in the CMS.
- Extensions and credits (DECISIONS 5 Oct 2026): a client's own data sits in `ext_defs`/`ext_values` on top of the core (never core columns), filled by SQL rules, an uploaded file or CeMO per row (`src/extensions/`), approved like any creation after a sample and an estimate, and queried as `ext_<key>` dimensions and filters joined by id in `src/query/builder.ts`. Long fills run in `cms_jobs` via `/api/cron/jobs`. Model work costs credits (`src/config/credits.ts`, placeholders; `charge`/`canSpend` in `src/credits/ledger.ts`); nothing is stopped until Fair sets `settings.credits.enforce`. Builders see `/credits`; Fair sees CMS → Credits, owners with real cost.
- Workspace onboarding (DECISIONS 5 Oct 2026): `workspaces.status` draft → loading → review → live → paused/archived; clients reach live workspaces only (`src/auth/live.ts`), crons skip the rest, only Refal or Rafli switch live. Data ops onboard a listening dump from the CMS (`/admin/workspaces/new`, `/admin/workspaces/[id]`): upload (Vercel Blob, or `ONBOARD_DIR` locally), inspect, brands (`brand_handles`, `brand_terms`), labels, load in sliced `cms_jobs` (`src/onboard/`; the TypeScript loader supersedes `etl/load_listening.py`, `etl/listening/*.json` is an export via `pnpm onboard export`), relevance preview/apply (`src/onboard/relevance.ts` is the rule), topics, health (`/api/cron/health`; `settings.notes` is what CeMO reads about the data).
- Learning (DECISIONS 5 Oct 2026): signals go through `signal()` (`src/learning/signals.ts`) to `model_events`, only kinds and payload fields whitelisted in `src/learning/kinds.ts` (ids, choices, flags, setting values; never text people typed, brand names or numbers from the data); a new surface adds its kind there. The browser may send only the `client` kinds (`/api/signal`). Insights (`src/learning/insights.ts`, nightly `/api/cron/insights`) are SQL counts in template sentences, from live workspaces with `settings.learning` not false, clients only, written only with three workspaces or more behind them (`src/config/learning.ts`). A version's origins and measures sit on `role_versions`; its before and after (`src/learning/outcomes.ts`) compares 28 days either side for workspaces that took it against those that stayed. The CMS shows them at `/admin/roles/[role]`, the version page and a workspace's Signals tab.
- Case workspaces (DECISIONS 7 Oct 2026): `settings.restricted_to` (emails) hides a workspace from everyone else, staff included (`actor.hidden`, checked first in `can()`). The labeller reads the case from `settings.label` (subject, about, context, languages, voices, post_off_topic; `labelContext` in `src/label/run.ts`) and the workspace's `topics`, and writes `topic_id`, `voice` and, for a post off the case, `relevant = false`; without `about` the prompts are unchanged. First: `kahf-threads` (Refal and Raissa only). A profile with `settings.dashboard = "reputation"` gets the Chorus dashboard and decks, its crisis view moves to `/pulse`, and earned posts with a stance count as voices beside comments (`VOICES` in `src/reputation/dashboard.ts`; panels unchanged). `settings.pr.hide` (status, reach, csat, intent) trims the PR dashboard and decks per workspace; where posts carry a stance, posts and comments are always shown apart (`Issue.split`, `conversation`); PR decks also take chosen dates (`YYYY-MM-DD..YYYY-MM-DD`) and can go day on day (`grain: "day"`, made each morning for the day before). Crisis slides (chronology, pace, motion, exposure, anger, moving; `src/reputation/case.ts`) and the Crisis Report template are on Chorus 1.2.
- Model (DECISIONS 2 Oct 2026): `claude-sonnet-5-5` everywhere (`ANTHROPIC_MODEL_CHAT`). It rejects a forced `tool_choice`: ask for one tool's answer with `toolAnswer` (`src/chat/client.ts`; auto, the tool named, one nudge), and leave room for thinking in `max_tokens`.
